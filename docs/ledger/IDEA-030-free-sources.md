# IDEA-030 N030-F — Free news and event sources

Checked 2026-10-10 SGT. User decision: **no paid news or licensing**. This narrows IDEA-030 R10's commercial shortlist for the requested implementation; it does not assert that commercial feeds are unusable. The companion [implementation prompt](../build/05-evidence-hardening-free-sources.prompt.txt) is prepared, not executed.

## N030-F00 — Recommendation and meaning of free

Use a small basket of official original disclosures: existing EDGAR, SEC news, Federal Register/BIS regulatory documents, DOJ releases, and European Commission releases. These add enforcement, export controls and competition-policy events without a subscription. FTC is useful once its retrieval path is reliable. This is not a free equivalent of a comprehensive Reuters/Benzinga wire: product rumors, supply-chain leaks, technical breakthroughs and Korean-language company news remain unevenly covered. No evidence here establishes net trading benefit.

Public access, zero price and reuse permission are different. An applicable open licence or express public reuse grant can be sufficient; a paid contract or a bespoke permission email is not inherently required. Attribute material and preserve the applicable terms and content version. Exclude material with contrary individual notices and third-party content not covered by the grant. Do not assume an RSS endpoint licenses a linked article.

The official challenge source, F-32/49/63/71, G-05/10 and relevant Q notes were rechecked along with the verbatim kit logging text. The competition permits authorized sources and requires complete model records; the external-source/market-commentary exclusions are team-policy questions. This follow-up does not reopen settled organiser questions or change trading authority.

### Scope of the overseas-use finding

Prefer express agency reuse policies or CC BY's international grant over a blanket argument from US domestic public-domain law. A [Commerce authorization template](https://www.commerce.gov/sites/default/files/authorization_to_publish_new.pdf) expressly reserves international copyright and offers recipient/work-specific overseas permission. It is not a blanket licence to LARP. Therefore **BIS regulatory text through Federal Register** has a better documented basis here than arbitrary **BIS newsroom content**. This distinction does not mean every US-government source needs a paid licence.

## N030-F07–F10 — Optional discovery and limits

- **F07 EFF original articles:** original material is CC BY 4.0 unless otherwise noted, with third-party exclusions. [Copyright policy](https://www.eff.org/copyright), [RSS directory](https://www.eff.org/rss). Potential supplementary technology/privacy/litigation reporting; distinguish reporting, advocacy and opinion. Its coverage is not a comprehensive financial wire. Lower priority than the original official-source adapters; no measured eligible-name recall or alpha.
- **F08 GDELT:** use only its permitted discovery/derived metadata within a documented field allowlist. [Project description and reuse information](https://gdeltproject.org/about.html). A URL, headline or event code does not grant the publisher's full-text rights, verify an occurrence, create independent corroboration or justify an automatic entry veto. Do not automatically crawl linked articles or assume every returned publisher-origin field is open-licensed.
- **F09 Wikinews:** archive-only. The [Wikimedia board announcement](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wikimedia_Foundation_Board_noticeboard/Archives/2026) and [resolved closure task](https://phabricator.wikimedia.org/T421796) establish the May 2026 transition to read-only. It cannot supply fresh Oct/Nov 2026 news. Historical per-version licensing and later migration hosts require their own checks; earlier project entries suggesting it as a live source are superseded on this point.
- **F10 OpenDART/issuer newsrooms:** free access does not settle the exact content, automated retrieval, hosted processing, retention and audit basis. [OpenDART terms](https://engopendart.fss.or.kr/intro/terms.do) and [search docs](https://engopendart.fss.or.kr/guide/detail.do?apiGrpCd=DE001&apiId=AE00001) were read, but no key was obtained or used; receipt date is date-only and a complete downstream grant was not established. [SK hynix newsroom terms](https://news.skhynix.com/en/terms-of-use/) restrict automated copying and noncommercial use; [Microsoft's document permission](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/terms-of-use) is limited to personal/noncommercial informational use. Do not generalize RSS or press-use permission to automated contest use. These are disabled/conditional, not prerequisites for the first free-source delivery. Press releases lawfully obtained through EDGAR stay on the existing EDGAR path.

## N030-F11 — Required data behavior and economic limits

Store source/publisher, canonical URL, licence/attribution, immutable permitted input, exact version/hash, publication-time precision, actual receipt, event/effective time, corrections and assessment completion. Record processing/translation changes and preserve original text alongside any translation where permitted. Raw model inputs needed for the audit remain outside Git; hashes alone cannot replace them.

For a proposed rule, the verified fact is that the authority proposed it. For a complaint, it is that the authority alleged or filed it. Sector relevance can justify assessment, but a rules document mentioning advanced chips does not establish a particular issuer's financial exposure or a signed return. Tie issuer relations to evidence and allow unknown direction. Keep duplicate releases from appearing to be independent confirmations.

Source connectivity is necessary but not sufficient. First measure unique relevant coverage, receipt-to-assessment latency, revised/missed articles and pipeline reliability. Then use IDEA-030 T01–T05 to test incremental complete-portfolio returns after executable costs. Feed success does not clear the verifier defects or the incompatible component contracts. Optional new news/hypothetical trading effects require their own economic decision; no policy is activated by this source list.

## N030-F12 — Verification scope

Primary policies/API documents were read by the source-rights reviewer and the lead independently cross-checked the principal reuse policies. Read-only, unauthenticated connectivity checks inspected a small number of responses for status, shape and timestamps. No credentials, paid services, messages, model calls, continuous collection, trades or corpus ingestion occurred. No article bodies were added to Git. A successful one-time response is not an uptime, historical-completeness or delivery-latency result. Observed on 2026-10-10; exact wall-clock receipt times were not recorded, so these checks cannot be used as point-in-time economic data.

[DOJ API documentation](https://www.justice.gov/developer/api-documentation/api_v1) specifies maximum page size 50 and cautions about more than four requests per second. Federal Register documentation caps pagination at 2,000 results; use bounded date windows for larger retrieval. OpenDART docs require a key, cap page size at 100 and constrain the no-corporation-code date range; the mentioned 20,000 limit-error threshold is not proof of an unlimited or fixed daily entitlement. Use source-specific documented limits rather than one invented global polling rate.
