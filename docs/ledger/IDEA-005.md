## Independent primary-source research

Accessed 2026-10-06. Sources below are research evidence, not official challenge rules. No unpublished numbers or estimates are upgraded to measured evidence.

- **S1 [PRIMARY; paper abstract and returned text checked]:** [Martineau, Rest in Peace Post-Earnings Announcement Drift](https://cfr.ivo-welch.info/published/papers/martineau2021rest.pdf), with [author's SSRN abstract](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3111607). Finds disappearance of analyst-earnings-surprise drift in all-but-microcaps since 2006, with sample extending through 2019. Supports a sceptical earnings-drift prior; does not measure every catalyst or this bStock portfolio.
- **S2 [PRIMARY; relevant full-text sections checked]:** [Lopez-Lira & Tang, v6](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.07619v6), introduction and transaction-cost discussion/Figures 3–6. Documents short-lived news predictability, concentrated in smaller stocks and negative news. The overnight strategy is unprofitable at 20 bp round-trip cost; value-weighted overnight returns are more fragile. The 6.54 → 1.22 Sharpe decline is reported. These long-short, daily-rebalanced results are not a direct estimate of four-name long-only veto returns. The page's arXiv header and manuscript version line differ; pin/hash the retrieved text for any future replication.
- **S3 [PRIMARY; abstract checked, not full-paper replication]:** [Luo, Ravina, Sammon & Viceira, NBER w34086](https://www.nber.org/papers/w34086), August 2025. Finds earnings drift conditional on retail contrarian pressure, including firm-size sorts. This is contrary evidence to a universal “PEAD is gone” claim; it does not establish net tradability for our names. The direct open failed, but the search tool returned the NBER abstract; no numeric result is inferred from it.
- **S4 [PRIMARY; original paper abstract/returned text and Microsoft explanation checked]:** [Deng et al., CUPED](https://www.exp-platform.com/Documents/2013-02-CUPED-ImprovingSensitivityOfControlledExperiments.pdf) and [Microsoft variance-reduction explanation](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/group/experimentation-platform-exp/articles/deep-dive-into-variance-reduction/). Pre-experiment covariates can reduce variance in controlled experiments. The gain depends on predictive correlation and estimator assumptions; using this citation alone to assign a gain to a stock panel is an extrapolation.
- **S5 [PRIMARY; documentation checked]:** [SEC EDGAR APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) and [SEC XBRL Guide, March 2026](https://www.sec.gov/files/edgar/filer-information/specifications/xbrl-guide-2026-03-11.pdf), public-float section. The APIs support standard-taxonomy entity facts, while frames select last-filed facts for calendar periods. Supports as-filed reconstruction rather than frames. Does not by itself verify historical universe completeness, delisted price joins or acceptance-timestamp semantics; these still need fixtures and coverage evidence.
