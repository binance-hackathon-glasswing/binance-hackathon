## R-W04-04 — first-submission recovery budget (2026-10-10)

Keith's [follow-up P2](https://github.com/binance-hackathon-glasswing/binance-hackathon/pull/6#discussion_r4237633348) is valid: eight absent pre-broadcast checks exhaust `attempt.probes`, yet first submission retains that exhausted counter and never queries later positive finality. The three prior corrections were independently verified in his follow-up review; this additional path was missing from those fixtures.

Correction: initialize `probes=0` and `next_probe=now` atomically with the **first** durable BROADCAST_ATTEMPTED transition. Require `broadcast_at=None`; no already possibly submitted state or restarted attempt gets a fresh budget. Prior absence-check events remain in the append-only journal. This is an execution-phase transition, not a restart-based reset or a change to the configured limit.

Preregistered pass criteria before running: (1) exhausted pre-submission checks for approve and swap still allow one guarded first submission and subsequent positive finality across restart; (2) failure of the first write-ahead commit preserves the old counter/state and causes no send, with the counter initialized only on a successful later commit; (3) lost first-send response persists the fresh budget and can finalize after restart without resubmission; (4) exhausted post-submission counters remain exhausted under repeated broadcast/reconcile and restart, with the wallet barrier retained. Four new tests plus the extended existing post-submission test must pass, alongside the whole offline suite. Commit/push before execution; no live signing/broadcast or policy change.
