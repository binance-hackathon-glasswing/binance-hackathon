- Hypothesis: one port-based approve/requote/swap machine can preserve immutable intent/attempt identity, fenced authorization, durable possible-broadcast evidence and a wallet-wide barrier through deterministic fake-venue faults and restart.
- Variants compared: uninterrupted fake execution vs crashes/storage failures/lost responses, stale authority, malformed payloads and provisional/reorged outcomes. No trading policy comparison.
- Data & periods: synthetic offline fixtures only, standard-library unittest and temporary SQLite database. No credentials, network, real RLP/signatures, production store, ai-seeds logs or realised fills. Fake signatures are explicitly unbroadcastable strings. SHA-256 identifies synthetic payloads, not Ethereum transaction hashing.
- Metric: deterministic assertions per boundary and state; no statistical performance inference.
- Pass criteria (frozen before execution):
  1. Successful flow requires approve finality, fresh quote and successful exact-payload estimate before swap; final success appears once and replayed methods do not resubmit.
  2. All applicable chain/wallet/nonce/token/router/recipient/amount/minimum/deadline/value/selector/gas mutations fail before signing; approval validation uses its own fields, without a swap expiry field.
  3. Market/health/audit/reservation/version/risk/expiry/ownership changes at sign/broadcast boundaries block side effects; persistence CAS checks authority and state version atomically. An emitted signature remains reserved for reconciliation.
  4. Persist signed bytes/hash before broadcast, and possible-send state before network; failures/crashes at those transitions never create a second attempt or skip a nonce. Idempotent fake signing reuses exact persisted bytes; changed bytes for an attempt ID fail.
  5. Pending/absent/timeout/nonce errors/expired quote and probe exhaustion do not clear the wallet barrier or reservations; unknown consumers/mismatched receipt identity and missing finality cannot finalize. Provisional inclusion can regress on a reorg.
  6. Final revert stops the economic intent; only positive canonical finalized success advances approve→swap or completes swap. Duplicate outcomes do not settle twice. Restricted signed bytes are absent from public event records; approve hashes cannot become swap order hashes.
  7. Restart preserves attempts, hashes, identity and bounded probe counts; concurrent creation/CAS cannot bypass the wallet-wide active-intent barrier. Live bundles and same-nonce replacement remain disabled.
