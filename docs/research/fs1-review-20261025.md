## 1. What FS1 actually does

FS1 seeks a five-name equal-weight book. Following PR279/F-125, the reviewed candidate applies its 95/60/30%
levels to **cash plus marked stocks minus the cost reserve**, excluding BNB from stock-buying capital. At
170 USDT cash and a 0.2 USDT cost reserve, the high target is 161.31 USDT total, or 32.262 USDT per name
(80.655% of a 200 USDT wallet before fills/costs). Actual funding and self-check fees change those figures.
The target remains subject to cash, costs, gas, venue, evidence and execution gates. It holds
through ordinary drift, tops up eligible shortfalls, sells on specified adverse evidence or risk requirements,
and liquidates at the team's terminal window. Increasing exposure increases both upside and downside; it does
not create stock-selection alpha. Five technology-sensitive names do not constitute broad diversification.

The deployed construction in `larp/runtime/service.py:build_driver` does not supply `ContextBuilder.series`.
`s1.evaluate_trend` therefore keeps its prior/start state with `TREND_INPUT_UNAVAILABLE`; FS1 starts UP. The 95/60
trend switch is not an active live price predictor. The deterministic exposure producer supplies Class 2 evidence;
it does not supply trade restrictions or suspensions, so its current inputs cannot reach the SEVERE 30% level.
Two eligible Class 2 events can reach CAUTION 60%. These are source facts, not a prediction of which events will occur.

Models extract and review filing claims, with the deterministic verifier and authority map deciding whether they
count. Verified Item 4.02 can force a sale; Items 1.05/2.06 can block buys for five sessions. That is a factual risk
overlay, not a calibrated probability of excess return. SKHYB's foreign-filer coverage remains asymmetric. Agreement
between models seeing the same filing is not independent market evidence or a calibrated confidence score.

FS1 uses initial-only gas funding: no agent gas top-up is enabled in these profiles. General 24/7 capability does
not imply top-ups occur. Five attempts per leg span the NYSE session, including a weekend; repeated failures can
exhaust the budget long before Monday. Twenty-basis-point slippage permits a lower authorized output; it cannot
guarantee execution, undo a rejection or establish that all earlier aborts were caused by price drift.

Checked rule sources: verbatim `docs/official/source_page.md` (scoring/costs/integrity),
`docs/official/kit/bstocks-trading-guide.md` (market state, prices, quotes), `web3-open-api.md`, SKILL F43–F64,
F95–F97, F114–F125 (including PR279's relayed activation message), K6/K8 and Q03/Q06/Q32/Q39. The page's equity-return formula is proposed; organiser F115 and
Q39 leave scoring details unsettled. Team PF04 windows are policy, not organiser deadlines. F119 settles24/7
permission, subject to the kit's openState guard. F120 settles first-gas funding; F125 specifies a 30 USDT
final-wallet swap unless organisers explicitly allow less. Neither mechanism nor the stated amount is an open
choice for the team. The 30 USDT buys a gas asset; it is **not a claim that 30 USDT has already been consumed in
fees**. Actual gas consumed and valuation of remaining BNB must be distinguished.

## 3. Formal reasoning and the limits of the checks

Let C be exact input cash, q_min the authorized minimum tokens, P the current valid mark per token, F the explicit
fee and G the profile's expected gas valuation. The revised BUY gate uses

    V_min = q_min * P
    c_buy = max(0, C - V_min) + F + G
    accept only if c_buy <= cash_cost_reserve and 10000*c_buy <= 50*V_min.

For any fill q>=q_min at this fixed mark, max(0,C-qP)<=max(0,C-q_min*P). Therefore the checked modeled cost is an
upper bound for admissible fill-output slippage. Decreasing q_min cannot improve the cost ratio. Calculations use
integers/Fraction, including token decimals and rounded-up gas. Actual gas can exceed expected gas; a changed mark,
revert or future exit cost is outside this bound. This is not a guarantee of realized net profit or a 50 bp hard
ceiling on every realized expense. Reservations still use the larger authorized gas action budget.

For a voluntary level reduction, V=qP and S_min is authorized minimum cash received:

    c_sell = max(0, V - S_min) + F + G
    accept only if c_sell <= cash_cost_reserve and 10000*c_sell <= 50*V.

The new reduction remains discretionary, with existing attempts, cadence, market state, expiry, nonce and signer
checks. Expensive or unroutable tiny residuals remain visible and pending. A 60% target is not a guaranteed 60%
holding when market/cost constraints prevent trading. Required Class 1/terminal/deep-cut sales retain their original
independent priority and minimum/cost bypasses.

Recovery is a hysteretic state machine. With peak H and equity E: H'=max(H,E); E<=0.92H' sets the buy stop;
E>0.95H' may clear it only under the trusted-state predicate; otherwise preserve it. E=0.95H' remains stopped.
The deep-cut bit is independently OR-latched at E<=0.88H'. No artificial peak reset is permitted.
Tests enumerate 6^4=1,296 paths across 88/92/94/95/96/101% of initial capital with serialization after each transition,
plus one-base-unit boundaries, unhealthy/missing inputs and other-latch preservation. The buy grid checks 13 spreads
× 21 slippage minima. These are bounded exhaustive checks and algebraic arguments, not an SMT/TLA+ proof of the
entire distributed system and not a proof that a stop improves returns. Full runtime/replay tests are separately
recorded in IDEA-093 and the review receipt.

For F-125 sizing, let A be cash plus marked eligible stocks, B the fixed-price BNB value and R the cost reserve.
The new stock budget is `level * max(0, A-R)`; the contract weight per name is this budget times its frozen
weight, divided by `max(1, E-R)`. Both policy and independent planner use that cap. With fully valued assets,
changing B while holding A fixed cancels out of the stock target (subject to ledger rounding), and the summed
targets cannot exceed deployable assets. At level 95%, the remaining 5% of A-R also leaves cash headroom for
entry costs. This does not override the loss ladder or promise every quote is executable.

A real PostgreSQL test constructs all five positions through settled synthetic buys, recreates the coordinator
between every leg, confirms the persisted 8% stop recovers, and exactly replays the journal with external ports
disabled. Separate tests check equal fundable targets, independent rejection of oversized policy targets and
absence of discretionary sales from BNB mark drift alone.

**Material remaining valuation issue (GAP-73):** startup and risk use the BNB price pinned in the quote summary,
not a current BNB mark. Thus a live BNB market move does not itself trigger the stops; it creates unobserved
equity error. At an opening 15% allocation, a 20% BNB price change is about 3% of opening wallet equity—equal
to the daily loss threshold. IDEA-055 B7 only bounds floor plus one top-up; it does not cap the actual seeded
balance. The old <=5% materiality justification no longer covers F-125. A fresh startup price can reduce initial
error but cannot fix later stale marks. Keith/Isaac/Keegan must explicitly accept fixed-price risk accounting or
implement and qualify timestamped BNB valuation before activation; changing an accounting price silently is
not part of this review. Score treatment remains Q39. No smaller gas swap is assumed.

## 4. What the research supports

Sources checked online 2026-10-25 SGT. Access labels distinguish full-text passages from abstracts. The implications
are this review's inferences, not the papers' claims about these five bStocks or this competition.

No cited result proves that every social or timing signal loses after costs. Equally, none establishes that a
particular signal will profit here. Adding an unvalidated sentiment threshold before freeze would create a new
strategy with unmeasured error, latency, turnover and data-rights requirements.

## 5. A concrete, testable path to predictive edge

**RV-H1, untested research hypothesis:** conditional crowd pressure may add value when paired with novel verified
fundamental information and the amount of price adjustment already completed. It is not enabled in FS1. Study one
fixed decision horizon, such as next eligible session through five sessions, truncated at new-risk/flatten gates.
Choose the horizon before inspecting outcomes. Avoid estimating dozens of weights on five correlated names.

Define point-in-time inputs: signed surprise versus a timestamped expectation; novelty versus previously observed
issuer statements; abnormal *unique-source* attention; signed imbalance and disagreement; realized volatility;
relative stock move minus its broad-market/sector component; and token/share basis with independent share/FX/ratio
data. Standardize from past data only. Retweets, syndicated stories, shared source text and multiple model opinions
on one document are one correlated information family, not independent confirmations. Missing inputs remain missing.

Compare three separately frozen hypotheses: (a) news-confirmed continuation with incomplete adjustment; (b) attention
exhaustion/reversal without new fundamentals; (c) the existing book with no signal. Include a price-only ablation to
test whether crowd data adds anything beyond the move that generated the posts. In this long-only setting a negative
forecast can mean hold cash or reduce an existing position; it never authorizes a short. Do not turn these cases
into hand-selected live thresholds without validation.

For a proposed change in exposure delta_w, expected incremental profit must cover entry, exit, gas, failed-attempt
costs and uncertainty, at feasible trade size. A conceptual robust decision rule is

    lower_bound(delta_w · expected_forward_returns) >
        incremental_round_trip_cost + uncertainty_buffer.

The comparison is against *holding the current eligible book*, not only against cash. Rebalance only inside the
existing risk/cash/cap constraints. For a two-outcome model with gain g, loss l and total cost c, positive expected
net requires p > (l+c)/(g+l); classification accuracy above 50% alone is insufficient. No numerical p, mu, covariance
or edge threshold is estimated in this review. With no trustworthy expected-return estimate, an optimizer cannot
identify a maximum-profit portfolio. More model votes do not fix that identification problem.

**RV-E1, evaluation design (not run):**

1. Freeze a rights-cleared, timestamped corpus and executable quote/fee/gas/latency snapshots privately with hashes.
   Preserve missing/stale periods and actual market schedules. Keep private training/label data outside Git.
2. Split chronologically into fit, calibration and untouched evaluation periods; purge training labels whose
   forward horizon overlaps later periods. Use calendar-time blocks across all correlated names, not IID ticker
   rows. Previously inspected EXP008 dates are exploratory, not a fresh held-out set for a new selected strategy.
3. Replay the actual policy/planner, minimum trades, paired approval/swap timing, retry limits, cash/gas reserves,
   terminal liquidation and both successful/failed costs. Compare equal-gross/eligible-name baselines so allocation
   beta does not masquerade as signal alpha. Include all-cash as a diagnostic, not an automatic competition default.
4. Report terminal wallet net, paired uplift, probability of profit, drawdown, turnover, coverage and calibration;
   keep external API/hosting costs separate. Use block confidence intervals and a documented multiple-trial
   correction; report effective non-overlapping sample count. Require positive lower bounds for both net return
   and incremental return, adequate coverage and operational reliability. Freeze minimum sample/power, confidence
   level and cost stress grid before data access; without those, no confirmatory run or promotion is permitted.
5. Validate prospectively in shadow with the same source/model latency; a synthetic pass, a few filing labels or
   one profitable development-wallet trade cannot substitute for economic evidence. Lack of power means
   INCONCLUSIVE. No adaptive threshold tuning during the scored run.

EXP008 remains useful exploratory evidence: its primary result favored a different four-name 80% EW comparator
over its simulated S1, with shared selected-name/proxy/cost limitations. It omitted minimum trades and used different
timing; its token check had only four non-overlapping windows. It is neither a test of this exact FS1 implementation
nor proof that its guard must be deleted. EXP007's pre-registered decision rule remains unchanged and unrun.
